SHERIFF_RULES.DOC_×
  1. Stop before entry. Every trade gets its DeathPoint before you buy, set just outside that coin type's normal shake-out range.
  2. Size from the stop. If you set a max loss, size = max loss ÷ stop distance. Never the other way round.
  3. First target pays you. On launches and fresh bonds, TP1 sells enough to take your whole entry back.
  4. Breakeven after TP1. Once TP1 hits, the stop moves to your entry. The rest of the trade can't lose.
  5. The runner rides a trail. What's left after the ladder follows the price up and sells on a drop of the trail distance from the peak.
  6. Time kills dead trades. No TP1 inside the time stop, you're out. Dead money is still a loss.
  7. Never move the stop down. Never add to a loser.
